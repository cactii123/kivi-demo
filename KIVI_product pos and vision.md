KIVI PRODUCT POSITIONING : ”Kivi is all ears so you can put your mind to work”.   
Like a bird perched on your shoulder, Kivi listens intently, and understands you meticulously and executes diligently so that users spend less time operating tools and more time doing the work that matters. 

KIVI VISION STATEMENT : Kivi aims to make your voice the most natural and powerful way to get work done helping you convey, create and perform your thoughts by listening to not just what you say, but also understanding how you work.  
Kivi is a personal AI assistant that boosts productivity. It goes beyond being a voice assistant, it understands each user’s language, intonation, preferences, context, and intent by listening between the words.   
Built with India’s diverse languages and dialects at its core, it boasts accurate dictation, translation and tone modulation features along with others. Kivi’s versatile styles, personalised shortcuts enable users to complete tasks seamlessly while still retaining their modus operandi. Kivi remembers what matters like from chat history-project titles, dates, places, people, keywords, understanding objective, specific preferences for different people , adapting to how each person works and using contextual learning to cut down instructions and execution time. By combining highly accurate multilingual dictation task assistance with contextual memory, Kivi makes task switching easier and reduces the friction between thought and action.   
Through its versatile features and intuitive interface, Kivi simplifies everyday workflows, enabling users to spend less time navigating tools and more time focusing on meaningful work.   
Kivi never assumes what it does not know. It asks when context is ambiguous, learns only from meaningful signals, and treats uncertainty as a reason to clarify and confirm.  
Kivi earns users' trust by making privacy and control foundational to every interaction. It never shares user data across apps, never assumes consent when accessing services like Gmail, Calendar, or Reminders, and gives users clear permission controls over every integration. As Kivi learns and assists over time, it aims to become a dependable bridge for completing everyday productivity tasks while ensuring the user remains in control of their data and decisions.   
As it learns a user's preferences, projects, and communication style, Kivi becomes increasingly personal and effortless to use over time.
